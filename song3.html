3.

Baat Karni Mujhe Mushkil

बात करनी मुझे मुश्किल

Poet
Bahadur Shah Zafar1775–1862, the last Mughal emperor
Form
Ghazalnine couplets
English translation
Found in Translationsix of nine couplets

This ghazal by Bahadur Shah Zafar is a poignant expression of longing, loss, and the passage of time. The poet reflects on how life, love, and companionship have changed, lamenting that conversations and gatherings, once effortless and joyous, have now become difficult and burdened with sorrow. The verses explore themes of restlessness, heartbreak, and the transformative power of love and separation. Through evocative imagery—of eyes casting spells, reflections illuminating, and chains echoing in solitude—Zafar captures a deep sense of nostalgia and despair. The ghazal resonates not just as a personal lament but as a reflection of the decline of the Mughal era, mirroring the poet’s own struggles and the fading grandeur of his time.

    poem & translation — in the poet’s order

    Urdu, in DevanagariEnglish translation
    1. 1

      बात करनी मुझे मुश्किल कभी ऐसी तो न थी जैसी अब है तिरी महफ़िल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      It was never this difficult for me to speak, Nor was your company ever quite like this before.

      महफ़िल mehfil
      — a gathering; here, the beloved’s company
    2. 2

      ले गया छीन के कौन आज तिरा सब्र ओ क़रार बे-क़रारी तुझे ऐ दिल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Who has stolen away your peace today, O my heart? You were never this restless before.

      सब्र ओ क़रार sabr o qarār
      — patience and calm
    3. 3

      उस की आँखों ने ख़ुदा जाने किया क्या जादू कि तबीअ'त मिरी माइल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      God knows what sorcery their eyes have cast, That my heart bends toward them like never before.

      माइल māil
      — inclined, drawn toward
    4. 4

      अक्स-ए-रुख़्सार ने किस के है तुझे चमकाया ताब तुझ में मह-ए-कामिल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      O moon, what secret light has my beloved’s reflection gifted you, That your radiance shines fuller than ever before?

      अक्स-ए-रुख़्सार aks-e-ruḳhsār
      — the reflection of a face
      मह-ए-कामिल mah-e-kāmil
      — the full moon
    5. 5

      अब की जो राह-ए-मोहब्बत में उठाई तकलीफ़ सख़्त होती हमें मंज़िल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Not translated on this page.

      राह-ए-मोहब्बत rāh-e-mohabbat
      — the road of love
    6. 6

      पा-ए-कूबाँ कोई ज़िंदाँ में नया है मजनूँ आती आवाज़-ए-सलासिल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Not translated on this page.

      ज़िंदाँ zindāṅ
      — prison
      मजनूँ Majnūṅ
      — Layla’s lover, the figure of love as madness
      सलासिल salāsil
      — chains
    7. 7

      निगह-ए-यार को अब क्यूँ है तग़ाफ़ुल ऐ दिल वो तिरे हाल से ग़ाफ़िल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Not translated on this page.

      तग़ाफ़ुल taġhāful
      — studied neglect, feigned indifference
    8. 8

      चश्म-ए-क़ातिल मिरी दुश्मन थी हमेशा लेकिन जैसी अब हो गई क़ातिल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Your deadly eyes have always been my foe, But never before have they been this lethal.

      चश्म-ए-क़ातिल chashm-e-qātil
      — the murderous eye
    9. 9

      क्या सबब तू जो बिगड़ता है 'ज़फ़र' से हर बार ख़ू तिरी हूर-शमाइल कभी ऐसी तो न थी

      Why do you turn against me, Zafar, time and again? This was never your nature, O my angelic beloved.

      'ज़फ़र' Zafar
      — the poet’s pen name, signed into the last couplet
      हूर-शमाइल hūr-shamāil
      — with the grace of a houri
